My agents wrote my posts. I posted none of them.

By Roko Grga · 17 November 2026 · 2 min read

Yesterday my AI agents planned my morning before my coffee was done. The Seneschal, the admin of the Vale, read the day's tasks, sent each one to the village that does that kind of work, and the marketing village wrote my posts for LinkedIn, X, Reddit and this blog.

None of them went out.

Where it got stuck

The work was done. The drafts were good. But they sat in folders. Each one was a file with notes on top and sometimes two versions to choose from. The picture for every post was a screenshot I was supposed to take myself.

So to post one thing I had to find the folder, open the file, copy the right part, take the screenshot, crop it, and then go to the site. Every step was small, and together they were enough to stop me. The agents were done and I was the slowest part of the system.

What changed last night

The To do in the Vale works differently now.

It opens by itself. The first window of the day is the To do, large, with my list on top.

Quickest first. Every task shows how many minutes it takes: 2 for copy and post, 15 for a recording, 90 for building something. I start at the top and the small wins are done before the coffee is cold.

Everything is in the task. A posting task holds the exact text in a box with a Copy button, the picture with a Copy image button, and a button that opens the place to post it. I copy, open, paste, post, tick.

The agent takes the screenshots. The Seneschal has its own browser and takes the pictures itself. Nobody asks me for one.

My list stays small. Only my daily tasks, what I wrote down myself, and the steps only I can do. Everything else stays with the villages.

The proof is this post

This post was on today's list. The text was in a box, the picture was next to it, and I pressed Copy.

The rule from day one hasn't changed: no agent posts, sends, pays or deletes anything without my yes. Posting is still my click. Now that click is the only thing I have to do.

What I learned

Agents doing the work isn't enough. If the last step needs me to search, copy and crop, it doesn't happen. The handoff has to be ready to use the moment I see it.

Tomorrow I'll see whether the list stays short.
