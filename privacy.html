Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The Vale is a personal tool made by Roko Grga and used only by him, on his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else. This policy covers the Vale app and this website.

What the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the Vale uses Google's OAuth to read the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, using read-only scopes (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly). It reads channel and video statistics such as views, watch time, average view percentage, subscribers gained and audience retention. It cannot upload, edit, delete or post anything.

How the data is used

The statistics are used only to measure the owner's own videos and to improve how future videos are planned and edited.

Storage and sharing

The access token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server of the Vale (there is none), sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

The Vale's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes the stored access too.

This website

This site is a static page on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics. The devlog video is loaded from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) only when you press play. Fonts, images and the trailer clip are served from this site itself. GitHub and YouTube handle their requests under their own privacy policies.

If you email me

The "Say hello" buttons open your own email app. Nothing is sent from this site. If you write, I (Roko Grga) receive your address and message in my Gmail inbox, use them only to reply, and delete them when you ask.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use, by writing to the address below. You can also complain to the Croatian data protection authority (AZOP, azop.hr).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
